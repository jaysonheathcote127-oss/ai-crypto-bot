## Turn an idea into a test

For each idea, record the source/date, market, timeframe, session/timezone,
precise entry and exit rules, invalidation/stop, sizing, holding period and
conditions in which the trader stays out. Keep descriptions such as "liquidity
sweep" or "strong trend" as unimplemented until there is an objective rule.
Distinguish a chart example or selected winning trades from a complete record.

Evaluate gold-to-crypto transfer explicitly: trading sessions versus a 24/7
market, weekend behavior, exchange-specific spot volume, dollar/USDT quote
differences, spread/fees, and data availability. Do not copy leverage, contract
size or point-value assumptions from gold products into crypto spot sizing.

Separate any useful market-context or risk-management idea from the entry rule.
Write an experiment protocol before results. The historical periods already
used here are inspected development data; they are not a fresh holdout for new
ideas. New prospective observations are needed for a genuine forward test.
